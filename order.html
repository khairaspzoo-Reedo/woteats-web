Three steps

How to order.

The same path people already know. The difference is what you are shown before you pay.

  1. Tell us where you are.
    Use your location, or pick a town. If you are outside the towns on the menu, we ask. Nearest kitchens come first.
  2. Find what you want.
    Search a dish, a cuisine, or a kitchen. Or ask Wot in a sentence. The plates on the screen are the offer.
  3. Delivery or collection.
    You see food, delivery, and the total. Card payment runs through Stripe. A pending order is not a paid order until Stripe says so.
